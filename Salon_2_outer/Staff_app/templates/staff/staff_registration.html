Staff Account Information

Staff Access

The new account will have staff privileges and can securely log in to the staff portal.

Complete Profile

After registration, the staff member should complete their professional profile and upload a profile picture.

Registration Guidelines

  • Choose a unique username for each staff member.
  • Use a valid email address for password recovery.
  • Create a strong password with letters, numbers, and special characters.
  • Staff members can update their profile after signing in.
  • Only salon administrators can register new staff accounts.

Ready to Register?

Please review all entered information before creating the staff account. Once registered, the staff member can log in and complete their professional profile.

Security Notice

Staff accounts should only be created by authorized salon administrators. Each staff member should use their own email address and keep their login credentials confidential.
